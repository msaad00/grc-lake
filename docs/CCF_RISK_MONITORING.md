# Risk prioritization and ongoing monitoring

These safeguards are proposed GRC Lake curations of the cited NIST outcomes. They are not NIST-published crosswalks, human-reviewed equivalence, certification, or proof of automated discovery. Existing collector coverage remains unchanged.

## SG-RISKPRIORITY-001 — Threat likelihood and impact prioritization

Identify potential threat impacts and likelihoods, then use threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood and impact to prioritize inherent risk.

Evidence: A current risk assessment links threats and vulnerabilities to affected assets, likelihood, business impact and the rationale for response priority. Production, customer exposure and internal use are explicit context where known; unknown values remain unknown.

Source: [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (CSWP 29)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf), CSF 2.0 Core, ID.RA-04 and ID.RA-05.

## SG-RISKRESPONSE-001 — Owned risk response and tracking

Choose, prioritize, plan, track and communicate risk responses.

Evidence: Risk response records connect each prioritized risk to a treatment decision, accountable owner, planned actions, tracking status and communication evidence. A suggestion without a selected and tracked response is insufficient.

Source: [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (CSWP 29)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf), CSF 2.0 Core, ID.RA-06.

## SG-AIMONITORING-001 — AI monitoring ownership and periodic review

Plan continuing monitoring and periodic review of AI risk management processes and outcomes with explicit responsibility and review frequency.

Evidence: A monitoring plan defines scope, accountable roles, review frequency, last review and next review, and links to current monitoring results. A live connection indicator alone does not demonstrate risk monitoring.

Source: [NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf), Table 1, GOVERN 1.5.

## SG-AIPOSTDEPLOYMENT-001 — Post-deployment AI monitoring and response

Implement post-deployment monitoring covering feedback, appeals, overrides, decommissioning, incident response, recovery and change management.

Evidence: Current monitoring and response records demonstrate implemented feedback evaluation, user appeals and overrides, decommissioning, incident handling, recovery and change management for deployed AI systems. Plans without implementation evidence are incomplete.

Source: [NIST AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf), Table 4, MANAGE 4.1.

## Evaluation boundary

The executable rule rejects missing or stale evidence and open findings. It does not establish semantic completeness of uploaded documents. A reviewer must check the stated evidence requirement and system scope before promoting a proposed mapping. Agents may submit observations or draft responses; recorded approval remains separate from deterministic evaluation.
